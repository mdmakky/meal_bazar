The short versionMeal Bazar is a free tool for keeping a shared mess's accounts. It works out figures from what the mess records; it is not a bank and does not hold or move money. Managers run the mess, members record their own entries, and disagreements about amounts are settled between the members.

Terms used here: a Mess is a shared household or group whose accounts are kept in the app; a Manager is a member with management rights in a mess; a Member is anyone who belongs to a mess.

2. What Meal Bazar is

Meal Bazar is a tool for keeping the accounts of a shared mess: meals, groceries, expenses, deposits and month-end balances. It calculates figures from the entries that members and managers record. It is not a bank, a payment service or a legal record, and it does not hold or move money.

A deposit in the app is only a record that money was given. Payment details such as a bKash or Nagad TrxID are text that people enter and managers can check; the app does not connect to those services.

The mess's entries belong to the mess. Disagreements about amounts are between the members of the mess: use the “report a problem” feature or speak to your manager. We do not settle disputes between members.

5. Accuracy and payments

Balances are calculated from what is recorded. If an entry is wrong, the result is wrong. Check entries, and use the report-a-problem feature when something looks incorrect. AI features only produce drafts; nothing is saved until a person reviews and confirms it.

Settling money between members, in cash, bKash, Nagad or any other way, happens outside the app and is the mess's own responsibility.
