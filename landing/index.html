Everything the notebook did, done properly

Try the phones yourself. Nothing here is saved, and every name and amount is made up.

Meals in seconds

Open the grid and tap a cell. It cycles 0, ½, 1, 1½, 2, and the totals add up as you go.

  • Guest meals and switching a meal off work the same way
  • Each member writes their own, the manager sees everyone's

Bazar: assign, tick, approve

Make a list and send one member shopping. They tick items and write prices in the shop, then send it as a bazar.

AssignTick and priceManager approves
  • A member-submitted bazar is added to the accounts once the manager approves it
  • Pick common items from the catalogue, or write your own

Deposits and shared costs, double-checked

Record cash, bKash or Nagad deposits. A deposit becomes final once the manager verifies it.

  • Mobile payments keep their TrxID
  • Monthly bills like electricity and wifi are split equally, or by a set share per member

Press Verify to see the manager's side.

A month-end bill everyone can verify

Tap a member on the Money tab to open the working of their bill: last month, deposits, food cost and the share of other expenses, line by line.

  • Meal rate = the month's food cost ÷ the month's total meals
  • Share the report as PDF or CSV in your group chat

One mess, two roles

For managers

The ledger is yours to run, and everything stays visible.

  • Create the mess and invite by 6-character code, QR or link, then approve join requests
  • Approve bazar and verify deposits
  • Plan the bazar duty calendar
  • Close the month. Reopening needs a reason and is logged

For members

Your own numbers on your own phone, without asking anyone.

  • Write your meals, or switch a meal off
  • Record a deposit and see when it's verified
  • On your duty day, take the list to the shop and tick as you buy
  • Open your own bill's working, line by line

Keep writing when the network drops

What you write is saved on the phone first and sent by itself when the network is back. A small strip at the top shows how many changes are waiting.

  • Switch the network off, then tap a meal on the phone: the strip counts what is waiting
  • A change sent twice is never counted twice

Your bill is calculated one way, in one place

  • Not on your phoneMeal rates, bills and balances are calculated in the database. The phone only shows them, so two phones never show two different totals.
  • Closed months stay lockedOnce a month is closed it can't be changed silently. Reopening takes an explicit action and a reason.
  • Every change is loggedWho changed what, and when, in an audit log.
  • Your data is yoursOnly members of your mess can see its accounts. Export reports as PDF or CSV, and delete your account whenever you want.

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. The APK is a free download from GitHub.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Entries are saved on the phone first and sent when the network comes back. A small strip shows how many changes are waiting.

Do I need internet to tick meals?

No. You can tick meals with no connection. When the network returns, the changes sync to everyone else's phones.

Is my data safe?

Only members of your own mess can see its accounts. That rule is enforced in the database itself, not just in the app, and every change goes in an audit log.

How do members join?

The manager shares a 6-character code, a QR code or a link. A join request needs the manager's approval.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Meal Bazar is Android only for now, as an APK.

Can I export a PDF or CSV?

Yes. Make the month's report as a PDF or CSV and share it to your WhatsApp or Messenger group.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, account deletion is supported.
