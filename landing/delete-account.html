Delete your account

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Operated by Md. Arafatuzzaman, Dhaka, Bangladesh

The short versionYou can delete your account inside the app in about a minute. Your name, phone number, photo and sign-in are removed and you lose access to every mess. The mess's own records stay, because other members' bills depend on them, but they are no longer linked to any account.

1. Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Meal Bazar and sign in.
  2. From the bottom menu choose More, then Account, then Delete account.
  3. Read what is removed and what is kept, type the confirmation word shown, then tap Delete forever.

If you are the only manager of a mess, make someone else manager first. Otherwise the app refuses to delete your account.

2. Delete without the app (by email)

Email makky.cse@gmail.com from your account's email address, with the subject “Delete my Meal Bazar account”. We will verify that the account is yours and delete it within 30 days.

4. If you own or manage a mess

  • Only manager: make another member a manager first, then delete your account.
  • To remove the whole mess, the person who opened it chooses Delete this mess in the mess settings, types the mess name and confirms. Everyone in the mess is told, and the mess with all its members, meals, bazar, money and notices is erased after 30 days. The owner can cancel any time before that, so back up the report (PDF or CSV) first.

5. Just want to leave a mess?

You do not have to delete your account. Open More, then Leave mess. If the mess owes you money, or you owe nothing, you leave at once and the managers are told. If you owe the mess, you ask the managers, settle up, and they mark you as left.
