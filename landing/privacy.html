Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Operated by Md. Arafatuzzaman, Dhaka, Bangladesh

The short versionWe collect only what a mess needs to work out its accounts. No ads, no analytics SDKs, and we never sell your data. Only the members of your mess can see your mess's records. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. About this policy

Meal Bazar is a mess-management app, operated by Md. Arafatuzzaman in Dhaka, Bangladesh (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, where it is stored and how you can have it deleted. Questions or requests (access, correction, deletion): makky.cse@gmail.com.

3. Who can see your data

  • Members of your mess can see that mess's meals, bazar, expenses, deposits, receipts and activity log. That is the point of the app: one transparent bill. Writing is mainly done by managers.
  • People outside your mess cannot see it. Row Level Security is switched on for every table in the database.
  • We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

5. AI features

  • AI only produces drafts, for example a meal draft from “aj Rahim 2, Karim off”, or a bazar draft from a photo of a ফর্দ. Nothing is saved until you review and confirm it, and AI never changes calculations or money on its own.
  • When you use an AI feature, the text or photo you provide is sent to Google Gemini, and to OpenRouter if Gemini fails. Their free tiers may use it to improve their models, so do not include personal details you do not need to.
  • Members are sent as codes (M1, M2…) plus only the names or nicknames needed to match what you typed. No account IDs, phone numbers or money figures are sent.
  • Our gateway does not store your text, photos or the AI's replies. It keeps only a usage counter.
  • The mess manager can switch AI off for the whole mess in the mess settings (it is on by default). The app works fully without AI.

6. How long we keep data

  • Your profile and sign-in details stay while your account exists.
  • Mess records (meals, bazar, expenses, deposits, receipts, log) belong to the mess and stay while the mess exists. Closed months are locked, and reopening one needs a reason and is logged.
  • If a mess owner deletes a mess, everyone is told and the mess is erased after a 30-day grace period, during which the owner can cancel. If someone asks us by email to delete a mess's data, we delete it permanently within 30 days.
  • Removal from backups can take up to 7 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and export: your mess's records are in the app, and you can make the month's report as a PDF or CSV.
  • Correct: change your name in More, then Account. A wrong entry in the accounts is fixed by the manager, and the fix is logged.
  • Delete: see section 8, and the Delete account page.

8. Deleting your account

In the app: More, then Account, then Delete account. Without the app, email makky.cse@gmail.com. The steps, and exactly what is removed or kept, are on the Delete account page.

In short: your name, phone number, photo and sign-in are removed. The mess's own records stay so that other members' accounts do not change, and they are no longer linked to any account.

9. Children

Meal Bazar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe we have, write to makky.cse@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Row Level Security on every table means you can only reach your own messes' data.
  • Receipt photos sit in private storage and are viewable only by mess members through short-lived links.
  • No secret keys are shipped in the app, and the AI gateway checks your sign-in and mess membership on every request.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable precautions.

11. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions or requests: makky.cse@gmail.com.
