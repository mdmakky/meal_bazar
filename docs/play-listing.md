## Full description — English
```
Mess accounts without the headache. Meal Bazar is built around the manager's daily routine: open the app, see today at a glance, tap the meals, add bazar or a deposit — done. About 2 minutes a day, in Bangla or English.

Tap, don't type
• A meal grid of members × meals — tap to cycle 1 → ½ → 0
• Long-press for meal off, guests or a custom count; guest meals are charged to the host
• Copy yesterday, or fill a member's row or a whole meal column in one tap
• Your own meal types and weights (e.g. breakfast = ½)

Automatic meal rate, with proof
• Meal rate, each member's food cost, shared costs, deposits and balance are calculated for you
• Every member can see exactly how their bill was worked out — no more arguments
• Close the month; balances carry forward

Bazar, expenses and deposits
• Bazar: date, buyer, total, optional items and a receipt photo
• Expenses: electricity, gas, WiFi, rent, maid and more — split by meals or equally
• Deposits: cash, bKash, Nagad, bank — with TrxID and screenshot
• Share a bazar summary to WhatsApp or Messenger

AI drafts, you confirm
• Type "aj Rahim 2, Karim off" and AI drafts the meals
• Snap a handwritten ফর্দ or shop receipt and AI drafts the bazar entry
• Nothing is saved until you review and confirm; AI never changes the money on its own
• Managers can switch AI off; the whole app works without it

Also included
• Monthly PDF report, made on your phone and easy to share
• Invite members by code, QR or link, with manager approval
• Multiple managers
• A log of who changed what, and when
• Bangla and English, optional Bangla digits, dark mode
• Free sign-in with Google or email

Privacy
No ads, no analytics, and we never sell your data. Only members of your mess can see its accounts. You can delete your account from inside the app.
```
